# Bhagya's Live Music Diary

A personal record of every show Bhagya goes to: what's coming up, and what Bhagya thought of the ones already seen.

## Language

### Shows

**Show**:
One concert, festival or music event Bhagya goes to, or might go to, on a date at a venue.
_Avoid_: Gig, concert entry, event

**Upcoming show**:
A show that hasn't happened yet.
_Avoid_: Future show, next show

**Maybe**:
An upcoming show Bhagya hasn't committed to. Every other upcoming show is a definite.
_Avoid_: Tentative, pending

**Past show**:
A show that has already happened.
_Avoid_: Attended show, old show

**Artist**:
Who a show is for: a solo artist, a band, or, for a festival or meetup, the event itself.
_Avoid_: Act, performer, headliner

**Pronoun**:
How the diary refers to a solo artist: he or she. Bands and events get wording that needs no pronoun.
_Avoid_: They (for a solo artist)

**Favorite song**:
The one song by the artist that Bhagya loves most, attached to an upcoming show so visitors can play it.
_Avoid_: A song I love, featured track

### Bhagya's verdict

**Score**:
Bhagya's whole-number verdict on a past show, out of 10.
_Avoid_: Rating, stars, ranking

**Perfect 10**:
A past show with a score of 10.

**Highlights**:
The songs or moments from a past show that hit hardest, in Bhagya's words.
_Avoid_: Setlist, best songs

**Wishlist**:
Songs Bhagya wishes the artist had played at a past show.
_Avoid_: Missing songs, requests

**Pull quote**:
The first sentence of what Bhagya wrote about a past show, shown large.
