# Bhagya's Live Music Diary

A personal record of every show Bhagya goes to: what's coming up, and what Bhagya thought of the ones already seen.

## Language

### Shows

**Show**:
One concert, festival or music event Bhagya goes to, or might go to, on a date at a venue.
_Avoid_: Gig, concert entry, event

**Upcoming show**:
A show that hasn't happened yet.
_Avoid_: Future show, next show

**Maybe**:
An upcoming show Bhagya hasn't committed to. Every other upcoming show is a definite.
_Avoid_: Tentative, pending

**Past show**:
A show that has already happened.
_Avoid_: Attended show, old show

**Artist**:
Who a show is for: a solo artist, a band, or, for a festival or meetup, the event itself.
_Avoid_: Act, performer, headliner

**Pronoun**:
How the diary refers to a solo artist: he or she. Bands and events get wording that needs no pronoun.
_Avoid_: They (for a solo artist)

**Friends**:
The people Bhagya goes to a show with. Not decided yet for most upcoming shows; nobody for a show Bhagya went to alone.
_Avoid_: Companions, crew, company

**Favorite song**:
The one song by the artist that Bhagya loves most, attached to an upcoming show so visitors can play it.
_Avoid_: A song I love, featured track

### Bhagya's verdict

**Score**:
Bhagya's whole-number verdict, out of 10, on a past show or on a punch.
_Avoid_: Rating, stars, ranking

**Perfect 10**:
A past show with a score of 10.

**Price**:
What Bhagya paid to get into a past show, or "free". Shown publicly.
_Avoid_: Cost, ticket price

**Highlights**:
The songs or moments from a past show that hit hardest, in Bhagya's words.
_Avoid_: Setlist, best songs

**Wishlist**:
Songs Bhagya wishes the artist had played at a past show.
_Avoid_: Missing songs, requests

**Review**:
What Bhagya wrote about a past show, in Bhagya's own words. Optional: most past shows get one, some only get a score.
_Avoid_: Write-up, notes, verdict

**Pull quote**:
The first sentence of a review, shown large.

### All-time lists

**Artists I've seen**:
Every artist Bhagya has ever seen live, from before the diary began until now, each with one punch per time seen. Kept in Bhagya's own order and spelling.
_Avoid_: Seen list, everyone I've seen, lifetime list

**Punch**:
One time Bhagya saw an artist live, like a hole punched in a card. It can carry the year, a score and the place, if Bhagya remembers them. Seeing the artist a show is for adds a punch; at a festival every artist gets one; openers never do.
_Avoid_: Sighting, count, visit

**Die-peacefully list**:
The artists Bhagya still needs to see, shown under Bhagya's own heading, "Artists I need to see so I can die peacefully". An artist gets a ✅ once Bhagya has seen them.
_Avoid_: Bucket list, wishlist (a wishlist is songs, not artists)

**Tag-along list**:
Artists Bhagya wouldn't mind seeing if the chance came up, shown under Bhagya's own heading, "artists i wouldnt mind tagging along to see". It can include artists Bhagya has already seen.
_Avoid_: Maybe list (a Maybe is an upcoming show), second list
