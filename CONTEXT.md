# Bhagya's Live Music Diary

A personal record of every show Bhagya goes to: what's upcoming, and what Bhagya thought of the ones already seen.

## Language

### The record

**Side A**:
The now side: a small player (songs from summer arc), the upcoming shows, then this year's past shows. The introduction opens from a button in its top-left corner.
_Avoid_: Home, main page, tab

**Side B**:
The all-time side: the die-peacefully list, the tag-along list, then the artists I've seen.
_Avoid_: Archive, second page, tab

**Introduction**:
Bhagya's own words, telling visitors from Instagram who Bhagya is and how the obsession started, exactly as typed. It has its own page, opened from the top-left button on side A.
_Avoid_: Bio, about, intro text

**Summer arc**:
Bhagya's Spotify playlist; a random song from it plays at the top of side A. The page never names it.
_Avoid_: Station, radio, mix

### Shows

**Show**:
One concert, festival or music event Bhagya goes to, or might go to, on a date at a venue.
_Avoid_: Gig, concert entry, event

**Upcoming show**:
A show that hasn't happened yet. Its section is called "upcoming".
_Avoid_: Coming up, future show, next show

**Maybe**:
An upcoming show Bhagya hasn't committed to. Every other upcoming show is a definite.
_Avoid_: Tentative, pending

**Past show**:
A show that has already happened. Its section is called "been there".
_Avoid_: Attended show, old show

**Artist**:
Who a show is for: a solo artist, a band, or, for a festival or meetup, the event itself.
_Avoid_: Act, performer, headliner

**Pronoun**:
How the diary refers to a solo artist: he or she. Bands and events get wording that needs no pronoun.
_Avoid_: They (for a solo artist)

**Friends**:
The people Bhagya goes to a show with. Not decided yet for most upcoming shows; nobody for a show Bhagya went to alone.
_Avoid_: Companions, crew, company

**Favorite song**:
The one song by the artist that Bhagya loves most, attached to an upcoming show so visitors can play it.
_Avoid_: A song I love, featured track

### Bhagya's verdict

**Score**:
Bhagya's whole-number verdict, out of 10, on a past show or on a punch.
_Avoid_: Rating, stars, ranking

**Price**:
What Bhagya paid to get into a past show, or "free". Shown publicly.
_Avoid_: Cost, ticket price

**Highlights**:
The songs or moments from a past show that hit hardest, in Bhagya's words.
_Avoid_: Setlist, best songs

**Wishlist**:
Songs Bhagya wishes the artist had played at a past show.
_Avoid_: Missing songs, requests

**Review**:
What Bhagya wrote about a past show, in Bhagya's own words. Optional: most past shows get one, some only get a score.
_Avoid_: Write-up, notes, verdict


### All-time lists

**Artists I've seen**:
Every artist Bhagya has ever seen live, from before the diary began until now, each with one punch per time seen. Kept in Bhagya's own order and spelling.
_Avoid_: Seen list, everyone I've seen, lifetime list

**Punch**:
One time Bhagya saw an artist live, like a hole punched in a card. It can carry the year, a score and the place, if Bhagya remembers them. Places are short labels: MSG, BK, NYC, Afro+, Stanford, Coachella, SF, Oakland, Lagos, Lost in Riddim, Barclays, Red Bull, or a spot like Prospect Park. Seeing the artist a show is for adds a punch; at a festival, only the artists Bhagya says they saw get one; openers never do. Each punch gets its own score.
On the page, the total is labelled "performances" (e.g. "108 performances").
_Avoid_: Sighting, count, visit

**Die-peacefully list**:
The artists Bhagya still needs to see, shown under Bhagya's own heading, "artists i need to see so i can die peacefully". An artist is crossed out (struck through) once Bhagya has seen them.
_Avoid_: Bucket list, wishlist (a wishlist is songs, not artists)

**Tag-along list**:
Artists Bhagya wouldn't mind seeing if the chance came up, shown under Bhagya's own heading, "artists i wouldnt mind tagging along to see". It can include artists Bhagya has already seen; an artist leaves it the next time Bhagya sees them.
_Avoid_: Maybe list (a Maybe is an upcoming show), second list
