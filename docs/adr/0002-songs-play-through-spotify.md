# Songs play Spotify's 30-second clip in the page's own player

Most visitors come from Bhagya's Instagram on a phone and aren't logged into Spotify, so Spotify only gives them its own fixed 30-second preview of each song; a page can't move that clip to the most played part. YouTube would play the full song from its "most replayed" moment, but it needs a visible video player and can show ads, so Bhagya chose Spotify's clip.

At first the clip played through Spotify's embedded player, kept out of sight behind the page's own bar. Phones only let that player start from a tap inside it, so it had to appear as a second player next to the bar, and Bhagya ruled that out: the bar at the bottom is the only player. The page now plays each song's clip itself (the `preview` link next to the song, from Spotify's embed page; `tools/clips.py` fills it in), which starts from the first tap on any phone. Everyone hears the 30-second clip, including visitors who are logged into Spotify; a song without a clip opens on Spotify instead.
