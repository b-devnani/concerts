# Songs play through Spotify, so most visitors hear a 30-second clip

Most visitors come from Bhagya's Instagram and aren't logged into Spotify, and Spotify only gives them its own fixed 30-second preview of each song; a page can't move that clip to the most played part (tested: seeking past it clamps to the end of the clip). YouTube would play the full song from its "most replayed" moment for everyone, but it needs a visible video player and can show ads, so Bhagya chose Spotify's clip. Visitors who are logged into Spotify hear the full song, starting at the most replayed moment.
