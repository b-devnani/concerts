# 4. Artists are "he" or "she", never "they"

**Status:** Accepted

**Context:** Bhagya asked for "he or she" instead of "they" when the page talks about an artist.

**Decision:** Each solo artist has a `pronoun` field (`he` or `she`). Bands, festivals and events have none, and their labels are worded so they need no pronoun.

**Consequences:** New copy about artists either uses the `pronoun` field or avoids pronouns entirely.
