# Glossary

The words this site uses, on the page and in `contents/concerts.yml`. When a design or a change talks about one of these, it means exactly this.

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Show** | One entry in `concerts.yml`: an artist, festival or event on a date at a venue. |
| **Upcoming show** | A show whose `date` (or `end_date`) is today or later. Listed under "What's next". |
| **Maybe** | An upcoming show Bhagya might go to (`status: Maybe`). Every upcoming show without it is a definite. |
| **Past show** | A show whose date has passed. It becomes a review card. |
| **Score** | Bhagya's rating, out of 10. A 10 is "A perfect 10". |
| **Highlights** | The songs or moments that hit. Shown exactly as typed. |
| **Wishlist** | Songs Bhagya wishes the artist had played. Labelled "I wish he'd played" or "I wish she'd played". |
| **Review** | Bhagya's write-up, exactly as typed. Its first sentence is the pull quote; the full text opens from "Read my review". |
| **Favorite song** | One song per upcoming show, playable on the page. Labelled "My favorite song". |
| **Crew** | Who Bhagya went with (`companions`). "TBD" for upcoming shows; "Alone" reads "I went solo". |
| **Pronoun** | `he` or `she` for a solo artist. Bands and festivals have none, and their wording needs none. |
